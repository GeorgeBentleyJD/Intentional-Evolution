The Lie of Disembodied Living

We’re living in a culture that treats the body like a meat vehicle. Something to manage, fix, or drag along for the ride.
But your body is not in the way.

It’s the one place where presence, instinct, intuition, and transformation converge.

Embodiment Is Intelligence

Let’s break the myth that the body is dumb.
Your gut has more neurons than a dog’s brain. Your fascia stores trauma. Your vagus nerve regulates every emotion you think is “just in your head.”
The body is a data center.
Every tension is feedback. Every ache is a request. Every sensation is a signal.
If you want to evolve, the first step isn’t reading a book.
It’s asking: *What does my body know that I’ve been ignoring?*
