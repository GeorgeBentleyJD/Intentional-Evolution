You’re not lazy. You’re not broken. You’re not out of time.
You’re just running too much unoptimized code.
Which brings us to Pillar #9 of the Intentional Evolution Manifesto:
Simplicity Scales. Drama Drains.
Most energy loss is not physical. It’s emotional bandwidth waste.
We love to blame fatigue on aging. But half the time, it’s not biology—it’s bandwidth.
Your nervous system isn’t overwhelmed because you’re old.
It’s overwhelmed because your life is bloated with tasks, decisions, expectations, and noise that don’t actually move the needle.

Drama Is a System

Let’s be clear: drama isn’t just interpersonal conflict.
It’s complexity that doesn’t convert.
It’s saying yes to the thing that already drained you last time.
It’s re-explaining, rethinking, re-justifying the things you should’ve already simplified or dropped.
It’s emotional churn masquerading as responsibility.
And it’s killing your momentum.

Simplicity Isn’t Laziness — It’s Leverage

Boomers were raised to believe “hard work” meant friction.
So what do we do?
We distrust ease. We resist flow. We build systems that reward over functioning.
But simplicity isn’t weakness. It’s wisdom.
It’s the art of knowing what actually matters—and building around “that.”

Simplicity is the new discipline.

Identify Your Energy Leaks
Ask yourself right now:
– Where am I solving the same problem more than once?
– What decisions drain me weekly that could be systemized or eliminated?
– What relationship or habit costs more energy than it creates?
– Where do I confuse effort with effectiveness?
The answers to those questions? That’s your next evolution.
Because you don’t need more energy.
You need less drain.

The Simplicity Protocol

Every 30 days, I run this ritual:
1. Write down everything that drains me—even a little.
2. Circle the things I’ve normalized.
3. Ask: “What would this look like if it were easy?”
4. Remove, delegate, automate—or reframe.
Complexity is addictive. But simplicity is exponential.

Final Thought
If you feel stuck, foggy, behind, or burnt out—try subtracting before you add.
Because most of the exhaustion you’re blaming on age…
Is just the weight of unsimplified decisions.
Let them go.
Strip it down.
Simplify to amplify.
Because simplicity scales.
And drama?
Drama just drains.
