Hook (Revealing) 
Everyone loves the phrase “hold space.” 
It sounds evolved. Noble. Like a spiritual badge. But in practice? It’s often a performance. A way to feel virtuous without doing anything. Or worse, a way to deflect accountability under the guise of presence. 
Because most people aren’t holding space. They’re hoarding energy. 
And that’s where energetic integrity comes in. 
Enemy Phrase: "Hold space" 
New Phrase: "Energetic integrity" 
The Contradiction 
We’re told to “hold space” to support others. 
But holding space can become a disguise. It’s used to stay passive, appear wise, avoid action. In some rooms, it’s used as a power move, a way to set the tone without taking a stand. 
Real integrity doesn’t look like ambient silence. It looks like resonance + responsibility. 
Scene 
In a coaching circle, a facilitator says, “Let’s just hold space for what’s arising.” 
But someone in the group is visibly in pain. No one speaks. No one moves. The circle stays still, performative in their witness. Afterwards, the person says, “That felt like abandonment dressed in empathy.” 
And it was. 
The Truth 
Holding space without intention becomes spiritual inertia. 
Energetic integrity means you don’t just witness energy, you direct it, regulate it, honor it. It’s not about silence. It’s about right-sized presence. 
If your energy makes the room feel heavier, foggier, or false, you’re not holding space. You’re leaking noise. 
Language Installation 
"Hold space" has been diluted into spiritual theater. It rarely means anything actionable. It too often becomes an emotional blanket tossed over discomfort. 
Energetic integrity recalibrates the standard. It asks: Is my presence amplifying clarity or confusion? 
It makes you responsible for your vibe, not just your volume. 
Evidence 
Somatic therapists and trauma-informed facilitators agree: presence without coherence can destabilize others. Just being there isn’t neutral. Nervous systems sync. Energy spreads. Integrity is not optional. 
Empathy without regulation = projection. 
Support without boundaries = absorption. 
Energetic integrity isn’t a mood. It’s maintenance. 
CTA 
Forget "holding space" today. 
Instead, check: What’s the actual impact of my energy right now? 
If it’s unclear, clean it. If it’s heavy, ground it. If it’s sharp, own it. 
Integrity isn’t invisible. It’s felt.
